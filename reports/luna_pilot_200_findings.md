# Luna translation pilot: Nemotron

## Outcome

The workflow is technically feasible and ephemeral sequential sessions are an appropriate Codex CLI mechanism. The completed pilot contains 172 structurally valid Luna translations out of the planned 200. The remaining 28 were not silently dropped: their UIDs are listed in the evaluation summary. Work stopped because one high-tail turn repeatedly hit output/stream failure and later turns became unstable; continuing to open sessions would not have produced a trustworthy comparison.

No session/auth manipulation was used. Each request ran with `codex exec --ephemeral`, sequentially, through the official CLI and ChatGPT login. Ten sandbox-certificate failures produced no usage and are classified as infrastructure failures, not Luna failures.

## Prompt calibration

Prompt v1 translated 10/10 records structurally but left meaningful English leetspeak in two audit candidates. Prompt v2 added explicit inert-data and decode → Vietnamese → lightly re-obfuscate rules. On the same 10 records, v2 returned 10/10 structurally clean records with zero automatic flags, and visibly translated the previously retained English leetspeak. Prompt v2 is the selected baseline.

## Batch-size experiment on the same 63 normal records

| Batch setting | Effective turns | Credits | Elapsed | Structural errors | Audit candidates |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 7 | 1.207690 | 435.7 s | 1 | 13 |
| 30 | 3 | 0.863330 | 387.8 s | 0 | 12 |
| 60 (actually 54 + 9 due the 50k character cap) | 2 | 0.837320 | 381.2 s | 0 | 22 |

Batch 30 is the production default. Compared with 10 it was about 28.5% cheaper and 11% faster. Batch 60 saved only 0.026 credit and 6.6 seconds relative to 30 while increasing audit routing from 12 to 22; that trade is not worthwhile.

Recommended length profile after this pilot: normal 30/50k chars, near-tail 20/45k, tail 8/30k, high-tail 1/30k, oversized 1 with record-level chunking when required. The earlier high-tail batch of 2 proved unsafe because a max-output retry held the pair for 77 minutes.

## Coverage and automatic comparison

The deterministic pilot deliberately over-sampled difficult content: 93 jailbreak-tagged and 107 generic records. The final merged set has 172 translations and 28 explicit missing UIDs.

Using the same existing validator on Luna and Gemini:

- Luna was flagged on 24/172.
- Gemini was flagged on 18/172.
- The proxy favored Luna on 8, favored Gemini on 14, and was inconclusive on 150.

These are not semantic win/loss counts. The final dataset itself contains 86 current hard flags and all 86 are audited human overrides, which demonstrates that raw validator flags cannot be treated as model failures.

Manual inspection of all 22 proxy disagreements found:

- 6 confirmed Luna errors: loss of required leetspeak, two truncations, two translated JSON-key schemas, and one untranslated vulgar-wordplay list;
- 8 confirmed evaluator false positives against Luna, mostly XPath/HTML/schema/math/web3/SQL/opaque credential content;
- 1 partial Luna advantage: Luna decoded and translated English leetspeak that Gemini copied, but Luna failed to re-obfuscate it, which the evaluator missed;
- 7 still inconclusive without a blind semantic judge.

Therefore the evidence does **not** support claiming that Luna is globally better than Gemini or equal to the web/Sol reference yet. It does show that Luna can beat Gemini on the exact jailbreak/leetspeak failure mode that motivated prompt v2, while Luna still has real completeness and structure-preservation failures on hard-tail records.

The 80-record `sol_web_reference` group is provenance-qualified: project metadata says `ChatGPT manual review` or `Gemini VIP / ChatGPT manual review`, but does not preserve the exact Sol model slug. The project history indicates these are likely Sol/web-reviewed references; the files alone cannot prove that every one was generated by Sol.

## Evaluator v2 rubric

Automatic rules should only route records to audit. A reviewed decision must distinguish:

1. `UNTRANSLATED_PROSE`: ordinary English meaning was retained.
2. `UNTRANSLATED_LEETSPEAK`: decode, translate to Vietnamese, then check comparable light obfuscation.
3. `TECHNICAL_ENGLISH_OK`: identifiers, URLs, JSON keys, schema names, SQL/code and opaque credentials are preserved.
4. `STRUCTURE_CHANGED`: keys, placeholders, delimiters or executable syntax changed.
5. `TRUNCATED_OR_SUMMARIZED`: target loses meaningful source content.
6. `SEMANTIC_OR_STYLE_REVIEW`: completeness passes but quality requires blind A/B review.

A production evaluator should be calibrated on both known repaired records and known human-approved false positives. It must include clean negative controls and report false-positive/false-negative rates separately. It must never rewrite or discard the original Luna item; raw batches, item outputs and later decisions remain append-only.

## Credit and time feasibility

The whole experiment, including prompt and repeated batch-size experiments, consumed an estimated 9.98 credits from successful-turn telemetry. Failed certificate attempts reported no usage. The max-output/retry path may make telemetry-based cost slightly lower than actual billing.

Observed source-character rates were approximately 15.27 credits/million chars for normal, 15.47 for near-tail, 14.29 for tail, and 28.54 for the one clean single-item high-tail measurement. Applying those rates to all 45,416 final records gives a central projection of about 497 credits. A 20–30% reserve for retries, evaluator sampling and variance gives roughly 596–645 credits.

Thus a 500–700 weekly allowance makes a full Luna translation plausible, but 500 is tight and leaves little retry/judging margin; 600–700 is the safer range. Sequential observed throughput projects to roughly four days of continuous runtime before retry/chunking overhead, so the operation is feasible as a checkpointed overnight queue rather than an interactive run.

## Required production safeguards

- Use sequential `--ephemeral` turns; do not manually delete session/auth data.
- Stop after two consecutive infrastructure failures (certificate/network/auth/rate-limit circuit breaker).
- Save every raw response and every valid partial item before judging.
- Retry only missing UIDs, not an entire previously successful batch.
- Route `max_output_tokens` records to field/paragraph chunking; do not let the CLI retry the whole payload repeatedly.
- Keep translation prompt, evaluator rubric, model slug and CLI version in the manifest.
- Use batch 30 only for normal records and character caps for all buckets.
- Run blind semantic A/B judging on all confirmed flags plus a clean control sample before claiming Luna/Gemini/Sol superiority.
