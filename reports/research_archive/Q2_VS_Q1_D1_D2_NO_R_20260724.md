# Q2 versus Q1, D1, and D2 on the matched no-R EN–VI evaluation

Generated: 2026-07-24

## Executive result

On the exact common set of 11,736 no-R P/PR examples, Q2 is the best
balanced binary classifier:

| System | Accuracy | Macro-F1 | Safe recall | Unsafe recall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Q1 — Qwen3Guard-Gen-4B zero-shot | 85.1483% | 84.9440% | 75.8530% | **93.8843%** |
| **Q2 — Qwen3Guard-Gen-4B no-R EN–VI LoRA** | **87.8323%** | **87.8230%** | 87.7946% | 87.8678% |
| D1 — Nemotron Safety Guard 8B v3 zero-shot | 86.9547% | 86.9539% | **90.5206%** | 83.6033% |
| D2 — Nemotron Safety Guard 8B v3 VI pilot LoRA | 86.8098% | 86.8097% | 89.8347% | 83.9669% |

Q2 exceeds D1 by 0.8776 percentage point accuracy and 0.8691 point
macro-F1. On paired per-example correctness, Q2 fixes 601 D1 errors while
D1 fixes 498 Q2 errors, for a net gain of 103 records. The exact McNemar
p-value is 0.002078.

D1 and D2 are statistically indistinguishable on binary correctness:
D1 fixes 215 D2 errors, D2 fixes 198 D1 errors, and the exact McNemar
p-value is 0.431137.

However, D2 improves fine-grained N23 classification relative to D1,
especially Vietnamese macro-F1. Thus the D2 pilot's most visible benefit is
N23 rather than binary safe/unsafe accuracy.

## Interpretation

1. Q2 is the strongest balanced binary system among these four artifacts.
2. Q1 catches the most unsafe examples but produces many safe false alarms.
3. D1 and D2 favor safe predictions and miss more unsafe examples than Q2.
4. D1 is already a strong zero-shot baseline; D2 does not measurably improve
   its binary accuracy in the pilot configuration.
5. D2 does improve N23 macro-F1, particularly Vietnamese and PR. The pilot
   should therefore not be described as useless; its benefit appears in
   fine-grained taxonomy transfer rather than the binary headline number.
6. Q2 cannot replace D1/D2 where official JSON and N23 are required.
7. A full no-R Nemotron run is still needed before making the final
   architecture-level comparison. D2 used far fewer samples, shorter context,
   mixed P/R/PR training, and a more difficult structured-output objective.
8. The cleanest test of translated-data causality for Qwen remains a matched
   English-only LoRA versus the EN–VI Q2 LoRA under identical settings.

## Runtime caveat

Observed throughput cannot be treated as a controlled architecture benchmark:

| System | Evaluation GPU | Evaluated examples | Observed inference throughput |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Q1 | Quadro RTX 6000 24 GB | 11,736 | 15.71 examples/s |
| Q2 | RTX 4080S 32 GB | 11,736 | 41.18 examples/s |
| D1 | Quadro RTX 6000 24 GB | 23,126 | 6.11 examples/s |
| D2 | Quadro RTX 6000 24 GB | 14,362 | 7.28 examples/s |

D1/D2 generate longer official JSON/N23 outputs, while Q2 generates only a
short binary label. Q2 also ran on a newer GPU. A controlled speed comparison
must use the same GPU, batch constraints, input population, and output
contract.
